Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026Developer: Chase RichardsonApp: Astryn Rift: Space Defense

This policy describes what Astryn Rift: Space Defense (“the game,” “we,” or “us”) stores and where it is kept. It is written to match how the shipped app works at launch.

The short version

  • The game does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party advertising SDKs. There are no ads.
  • You do not create an account. There is no chat and no online profile service.
  • Your progress, settings, and callsign are saved on your device, and in your own iCloud or Google Play Games storage when that sync is on. We cannot access or read that storage, and we do not collect it.
  • If you buy Lumarite, payment is handled by Apple or Google. We never see or store your card details. Lumarite is spent on cosmetics and a permanent 1.5× Speed Boost unlock; weapons and upgrades are earned in play with alloy.

Who we are

Astryn Rift: Space Defense is published by Chase Richardson, an individual developer. The app’s bundle identifier is com.astrynrift.

Questions about this policy:

astrynrift.support@gmail.com

Where your save is stored

Game data is saved on the device you are playing on. The game also keeps local backup copies of that save on the same device so a damaged file can be recovered. On iPhone and iPad, the same save syncs automatically through iCloud, using the Apple ID already signed into the device — there is no separate sign-in screen in the game. On Android, it syncs through Google Play Games saves, using the Google account on the device. That copy lives in your own iCloud or Google storage. We cannot access or read it, and we do not collect it. If iCloud (or Google Play Games) is turned off, the save stays on that device only. The game does not use cookies, and it does not use session storage or IndexedDB for progress.

We do not operate our own cloud-save or player-account service, and we cannot access or read the copy in your iCloud or Google storage. Sign-in accounts of ours, if added later (for example with leaderboards), will be described here first. Deleting the app or clearing the app’s data on one device does not erase a synced copy, so the save can come back when you reinstall and are signed into the same Apple ID or Google account, as long as sync was on. If iCloud or Google Play Games is off, uninstalling or clearing the app erases the save on that device. Settings → Reset all progress erases the save, including the synced copy, so it will not come back on your other devices.

What we do not collect

The game does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, precise or coarse location, advertising identifiers, or other personal information for analytics or marketing. We do not sell or share personal information. We do not use the game to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. A copy of your save may be stored in iCloud or Google Play Games, but that storage belongs to you and is controlled by Apple or Google. We cannot access or read it, and it is not data we collect.

In ordinary play, the game loads its own sound and other local game assets. It may also sync your save with iCloud or Google Play Games. There are no analytics beacons and no advertising networks.

In-app purchases

Optional real-money purchases are four consumable Lumarite packs, sold in the Lumarite Exchange:

  • Shard Pack — $2.99 — 300 Lumarite
  • Sealed Case — $9.99 — 1,050 Lumarite
  • Supply Crate — $19.99 — 2,200 Lumarite
  • Reserve Vault — $29.99 — 3,600 Lumarite

Lumarite is spent in the game on cosmetics (skins, trails, paints, decals, Mantles, and similar looks) and on a permanent 1.5× Speed Boost unlock, which costs 300 Lumarite. Speed Boost does not change weapon power or other combat stats; it lets you run matches at 1.5× speed. Weapons and upgrades are earned in play with alloy. Lumarite is not required to play or to finish the campaign. Skins and bundles are bought with Lumarite, not with a separate real-money product.

Payment, billing, taxes, and refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play under Apple’s or Google’s terms. We never receive your payment card number, bank details, or full store account credentials.

How purchases are confirmed

On iPhone and iPad, purchases are confirmed on the device with Apple’s StoreKit 2. The game does not send receipts, transaction IDs, or the local player ID to a server of ours. A record of product and transaction IDs already applied stays on your device, and in your iCloud save when sync is on, so the same pack is not granted twice.

If we later add a receipt-verification server, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that change ships. That request would include the store platform, the product ID, the receipt, the transaction ID, and the anonymous local player ID described above — not your name, email, or card data — and would be used only to fulfill the purchase and to prevent the same transaction from being granted twice.

Bug reports

If you choose Settings → Report a bug, the game opens an email draft to our support address. The draft can include the app version, a general device/OS string, screen size, and the time of the report, along with anything you type. Nothing is sent unless you send the email. You can also write to us directly without using that button.

Children

The game is a general-audience tower-defense game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not ask for a name, email, or other contact details as a condition of play (your optional callsign is stored with your save). Purchases on a child’s device are controlled by Apple or Google parental controls, such as Ask to Buy.

Your choices

  • Start over: Settings → Reset all progress. This erases the save, including the copy that syncs through iCloud or Google Play Games.
  • Uninstalling the app does not erase a synced copy. If iCloud or Google Play Games is off, uninstalling or clearing the app’s data erases the save on that device.
  • Restore store purchases that belong to your Apple or Google account: Settings → Purchases → Restore. See the Support page for what restore can and cannot do.
  • Questions about privacy or a purchase can be sent to the support address in Contact below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. If a receipt-verification server or any other collection is added, we will describe it here before that change ships.
