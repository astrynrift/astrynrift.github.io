Frequently asked questions

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore next to Purchases. The store will check the Apple ID or Google account signed in on that device and apply any purchases again that can be restored.

The only real-money items are the four Lumarite packs (Shard Pack, Sealed Case, Supply Crate, and Reserve Vault). These are consumable. A pack you already received and spent is not granted again. Cosmetics bought with Lumarite, and the permanent 1.5× Speed Boost unlock, are part of your save — including the iCloud copy on iPhone and iPad — and are not re-granted by Restore. If you were charged and the Lumarite never appeared, try Restore once, then contact us with the approximate time of the purchase and the pack name. Apple or Google handle refunds.

What does Lumarite buy?

Lumarite is the optional premium currency. You can spend it on cosmetics (skins, trails, paints, decals, Mantles, and similar looks) and on a permanent 1.5× Speed Boost unlock, which costs 300 Lumarite — the same amount as the Shard Pack. Speed Boost does not change weapon power or combat stats; it lets you run matches at 1.5× speed. Weapons and upgrades are earned in play with alloy. You do not need Lumarite to play or to finish the campaign.

You can open the Lumarite Exchange from the Hangar by tapping + next to the Lumarite balance. The Speed Boost chip is in the Hangar header, beside that control. The unlock is stored with your save, including iCloud, not as a separate App Store product.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device, including your callsign, unlocks, and Lumarite balance. On iPhone and iPad it also syncs automatically through iCloud, using the Apple ID already signed into the device — there is no sign-in screen in the game. On Android it syncs through Google Play Games saves, using the Google account on the device.

Your game will follow you to other devices signed into the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, or the same Google account on Android. If iCloud (or Google Play Games) is off, progress stays on that device only. We do not operate our own account or cloud-save service, and we cannot read the copy in your iCloud or Google storage.

If sync is on, deleting the app does not erase that copy, so your progress can come back when you reinstall and are signed into the same Apple ID or Google account. If iCloud or Google Play Games is off, deleting the app or clearing its data erases the save on that device. Settings → Reset all progress erases the save, including the synced copy, so it will not come back on your other devices.

How do I report a bug?

In the game, open Settings and choose Report a bug. That opens a blank email draft to astrynrift.support@gmail.com that you write and send yourself. Nothing is collected automatically. Screenshots help when the problem is visual. You can also write to us directly without using that button.

I want to turn off purchases or reset the game

Purchases can be restricted with Screen Time or parental controls on the device. To wipe the game and start over, use Settings → Reset all progress. That erases the save, including the copy that syncs through iCloud or Google Play Games, and does not automatically refund purchases. Deleting the app alone does not erase a synced save.

Do you have ads, accounts, or chat?

No. The game has no ads, no tracking, no player accounts, and no chat. Lumarite can be spent on optional cosmetics and on a permanent 1.5× Speed Boost. Weapons and upgrades are earned with alloy.
