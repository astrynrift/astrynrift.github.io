Astryn Rift: Space Defense

A landscape 2D space tower-defense game. These are the official privacy and support pages for the App Store and Google Play listing.

The game is built by Chase Richardson. Progress is saved on your device and can sync across your other devices through iCloud or Google Play Games. There are no accounts of ours, no ads, no tracking, and no chat. Optional real-money purchases are four Lumarite packs, handled by Apple or Google. Lumarite buys cosmetics and a permanent 1.5× Speed Boost; weapons and upgrades are earned in play with alloy.
